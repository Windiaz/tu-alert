# Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Setup before going on stage: the laptop is on `/admin` and projected, 2–3 member iPhones have TU Alert open from the Home Screen, and each phone has tapped "Turn on alerts".

1. **The problem (20 s).** "When the alarm goes off, most of us think it's a drill. When something breaks, our email disappears into silence."
2. **Report (40 s).** A member takes a photo with the iPhone, picks *Air conditioning*, picks Engineering Bldg 2, Floor 3, then Submit. The report appears on the projected admin queue.
3. **Status (30 s).** The admin sets it to Acknowledged, then In progress. The audience sees the phone update live on the member's screen (hold it up or mirror it).
4. **Confirmed alert (50 s).** The admin creates a *Confirmed incident* for Engineering Building 2, confirms "Is this real?", and sends. Every phone turns red: "CONFIRMED INCIDENT — NOT A DRILL". The members tap I'm safe, and the admin counter goes 1, 2, 3.
5. **Drill (20 s).** The admin sends a Scheduled drill notice. The phones show the calm blue "no hazard" screen. "Two tiers, so the real one is never ignored."
6. **Close (20 s).** "Free pilot for one faculty, then THB 15,000–30,000 a year. Students never pay."

Backup: if anything fails, play the video recorded on 11 Nov and keep talking.
